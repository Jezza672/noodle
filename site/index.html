Wire up sound.

Noodle is a node-based DAW. Build instruments and mixes by connecting nodes, then arrange them on a timeline.

Read the sourceBuild from source
Open source, GPL-3.0. Built in Rust.

Patch

Wire nodes into instruments, effects and mixes, like Blender's compositor. CLAP and VST3 plugins sit in the graph as nodes too.

Arrange

Lay clips out on tracks along a timeline, with tempo and meter changes, markers and time warping.

Edit

Write notes in a piano roll, shape velocity and controller lanes, and play ideas in live from a MIDI keyboard.

Open source, built in Rust
