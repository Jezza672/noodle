# MIDI learn and several MIDI inputs

"MIDI learn and MIDI output", batches 1 and 2 (issue #293). MIDI output and clock out are still open in [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md). The design is in [../architecture/midi-and-recording.md](../architecture/midi-and-recording.md).

- **Several inputs.** `AudioConfig::midi_inputs` replaces the single `midi_input`. The audio settings dialog lists every port with a checkbox (a chosen port that is unplugged stays listed as "not found"). The session opens a connection per chosen port, adds and removes ports one at a time, and `watch_midi` looks at each about once a second: an unplugged port is let go (releasing only its own held notes) and reopened when it returns.
- **Ports on the bus.** Messages carry their source port; `MidiBus::subscribe_port` hears one port (or all). `MIDI In` and `MIDI Control` have a `port` config, empty or `*` meaning every open port. The computer keyboard, which has no port, reaches the "all" listeners.
- **Learn MIDI.** Right-click a parameter (in the graph or the properties panel) and choose Learn MIDI, then move a control on any open port. One undo step adds a `MIDI Control` in the parameter's group with that port and controller (a controller, pitch bend or channel pressure; notes and channel mode messages are ignored) and wires it in. The range, taper and starting position are copied once from the target by the same rule as Automate (an offsetting parameter gets -1 to 1, starting in the middle). Refused on a wired parameter. On an Automation node it wires the `record` input. Escape, or choosing the entry again, stops listening.
- **`MIDI Control`.** New parameters `encoder` (absolute, or relative as two's complement, sign-magnitude or binary offset, one step = 1/128 of the travel), `pickup` (jump, or wait until the knob reaches or passes the held value), `start`, `min`, `max`, `taper`. A `port` config makes it a source of its own; empty keeps reading the `in` events wire only.

Tests: bus filtering and the tap in `noodle-io/src/midi.rs`; the three codings, both ends of the travel, pickup from below and above, the range and the per-port reads in `noodle-nodes/src/control.rs`; Learn on a synth group's cutoff (node, parent, port, controller, wire, one undo), on an armed Automation node (`record`), and stopping, in `session/learn_midi.rs`; several ports opening and unplugging in `session.rs`; the checklist in `devices.rs`.
