# Feedback regions

Wired feedback loops as tight as the patch asks, with no setting (#547, ADR 0013).

- **Regions and chunks.** The compiler groups the nodes on a loop into a region, kept together in the schedule; the plan runs them in chunks as short as the loop's shortest delay (`Node::loop_chunk`). Events are windowed and rebased per chunk, silence flags are per chunk inside the region and ANDed outside, meters and CPU time are per block, and a region is one step in the parallel step graph. A region with a plugin, a spectral node or a compensation line runs whole blocks as before. `Delay` loses its one-block floor inside a region, so a loop's sound no longer depends on the engine's block size. Architecture: [../architecture/feedback-and-silence.md](../architecture/feedback-and-silence.md).
- **Feedback node** (`noodle.util.feedback`): a one-sample delay that is a loop breaker, so delay-free loops (feedback FM, filters from integrators) are legal. The `Loop` diagnostic now suggests it.
- **Templates** with three presets each, made from primitives: Plucked String (Voices, a noise burst, a loop of Delay tuned by `1 / pitch` less the filter's lag, a low-pass for damping, a loss in decibels per second so a high note rings as long as a low one), Comb and Flanger. Examples `plucked-string.ron` and `feedback-fm.ron` with golden renders. The three sound different from nothing on main, so only `DSP_GENERATION` (now 9) changed for old loops.
- **Tests.** A loop rings within 0.05 samples of its delay (880 Hz); the same loop at engine blocks of 16 to 512 is identical; feedback FM matches a sample-by-sample reference at blocks of 16, 64 and 256; a note inside a region lands on its sample; the string follows the key across four octaves within 3 cents (36 to 84); a comb follows its frequency input to two samples up to 1.7 kHz; `realtime.rs` covers a region through plan swaps, seeks and delay sweeps; `parallel.rs` is bit-identical with a loop in the plan. Mutations that failed the tests: forcing the chunk to the whole block, not rebasing events, a two-sample Feedback.
